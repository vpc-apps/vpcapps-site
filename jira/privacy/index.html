Privacy policy

Privacy policy: Go/No-Go Release Scorecard for Jira

Last updated 8 November 2026.

This policy covers the app named above (“the app”), offered on the Atlassian Marketplace by VPC Apps, a brand of Venture Path Consulting, a sole proprietorship registered in India, Door No 39-5-40, Muralinagar, Visakhapatnam 530007, Andhra Pradesh, India (“we”). The website has its own policy.

Summary

  • The app shows you a report built from your Jira data, in your browser.
  • That data does not come to us. We have no servers for this app.
  • The app saves only your project’s go/no-go rule settings, in Atlassian’s storage for your site.

What the app reads

When you open the app, your browser reads the following from your Jira site, using your own Jira permissions:

  • The project’s name, issue types and releases.
  • For the defects in the chosen release, and in the previous release when there is one: key, summary, status, priority, type, assignee display name, dates, releases, attachment list, description and change history.

The app uses this to calculate the report on screen. It is not saved, logged or sent anywhere. When you close the page, the report is gone unless you have printed or downloaded it.

What the app stores

If a project admin or Jira admin changes the go/no-go limits, the app saves, under the project’s Jira ID: each rule’s identifier, its two limits, whether it is counted as a number or a percentage, and the criteria template it came from. This is kept in Atlassian Forge hosted storage for your Jira site. It contains no issue content and no personal data, and the app does not record who changed it.

To decide whether you may change the limits, the app asks Jira about your own permissions on that project each time. This check runs on Atlassian’s platform, and its answer is not saved.

Where data is processed

The app runs entirely on Atlassian’s platform and in your browser. Atlassian hosts the app and its storage, and stores data in the location of your Jira site. Atlassian’s handling is described in Atlassian’s privacy policy. We use no other providers for the app, and we do not share your data, because we do not receive it.

The app’s dashboard-gadget icon is an image file hosted on vpcapps.net. When Jira shows it, your browser fetches it like any image on the web; no Jira data is sent with that request.

The app has no analytics, tracking, advertising or AI features.

Files you create

Print summary, Print full report and Download shareable report create a file on your device that can include defect keys, summaries, priorities and assignee names. You control that file and who receives it.

Purchases

If your site pays for the app, you buy through Atlassian, which handles billing. Atlassian may give us business details about the licence, such as the site and the technical and billing contacts named on it, through its partner reports. We use those only to run our business, support you and meet legal duties.

Support email

If you email support@vpcapps.net, we use your message and address to answer you, and delete the conversation when it is no longer needed. Please do not send customer data or real defect content.

Retention and deletion

Rule settings stay until you change them or uninstall the app. After uninstalling, Atlassian’s hosted storage keeps the data for 28 days and then removes it. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing further for us to delete, other than support emails.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU or UK GDPR, California’s CCPA or India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its use. For data in your Jira site, contact your Jira administrator, who controls it. For support emails, write to us at support@vpcapps.net and we will answer within 30 days.

Children

The app is for business use and is not directed at children.

Changes and contact

We will post changes on this page with a new date, and update it before the app handles data in any new way. Contact: support@vpcapps.net, or by post to Venture Path Consulting, Door No 39-5-40, Muralinagar, Visakhapatnam 530007, Andhra Pradesh, India.
