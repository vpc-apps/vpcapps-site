Privacy policy

Website privacy policy · last updated 8 November 2026

This policy covers the website vpcapps.net and the browser games played on it. Each of our Android apps has its own privacy policy, linked from its game page and its Google Play listing.

Summary

  • We don't ask you for any personal information, and there are no accounts.
  • The website counts anonymous page visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. There is no advertising.
  • Browser games save your progress in your own browser (local storage). It never reaches us.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any web server, GitHub receives your IP address and browser details when you load a page, and may keep them in its logs for security purposes. See GitHub's privacy statement. Our domain name is registered with Cloudflare, which answers address lookups for the domain; the pages themselves are not served through Cloudflare.

Visit counter (Cloudflare Web Analytics)

We count page visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, run by Cloudflare, Inc. When a page loads, a small script from static.cloudflareinsights.com sends Cloudflare the page address (without anything after a "?"), the site you came from, page-load timings and your browser and device type. Cloudflare also receives your IP address, as any server does, and reports the visit's country to us. We only see totals, never individual visitors. The counter sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser (we checked in Chrome on 7 November 2026), and it never sees the contents of files you open in our tools. Ad blockers may block it; the site works the same either way. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Browser games

When you play a game on this site, it stores your progress (levels unlocked, stars, settings) in your browser's local storage on your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing your browser's site data. Some game demos are loaded from our older site, vpc-apps.github.io, which is also hosted by GitHub Pages and run by us under the same rules.

Free tools

Our tools at vpcapps.net/tools (such as CSV to Dashboard) process the files you choose locally in your browser and send nothing from them to us or anyone else; your files never leave your device. The tool pages count visits like the rest of the site (see Visit counter), and that count never includes your file, its name or its contents.

Videos and images

All videos, images and fonts are served from this site. We don't embed third-party players or social media widgets.

Advertising

This website does not show ads today. If we add ads in the future (for example Google AdSense in or around our browser games), we will update this policy before they go live, explain which provider is used and what it collects, and offer the consent choices required in your region.

Email

If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply, and we delete the conversation when it is no longer needed.

Children

Our games are made for a general audience aged 13 and over and are not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes and contact

We'll post any change on this page with a new date. Questions or requests: vpconsulting.apps@gmail.com.
