Privacy policy

Privacy policy for the website, tools and apps · last updated 16 November 2026

This policy covers the website vpcapps.net, the browser games played on it, our free tools, and three marketplace apps: the Sheet to Dashboard Report add-on for Google Sheets, the Release Readiness Scorecard app for monday.com and the CSV to Dashboard Report Actor on Apify. Each of our Android apps has its own privacy policy, linked from its game page and its Google Play listing, and our Jira app has its own policy. VPC Apps is a brand of Venture Path Consulting (India).

Summary

  • We don't ask you for any personal information, and there are no accounts.
  • The website counts anonymous page visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. There is no advertising.
  • Browser games save your progress in your own browser (local storage). It never reaches us.
  • Our tools, add-ons and marketplace apps work on your data inside your browser or inside the platform you use them on (Google Sheets, monday.com, Apify). We have no server that receives your files or your data.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any web server, GitHub receives your IP address and browser details when you load a page, and may keep them in its logs for security purposes. See GitHub's privacy statement. Our domain name is registered with Cloudflare, which answers address lookups for the domain; the pages themselves are not served through Cloudflare.

Visit counter (Cloudflare Web Analytics)

We count page visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, run by Cloudflare, Inc. When a page loads, a small script from static.cloudflareinsights.com sends Cloudflare the page address (without anything after a "?"), the site you came from, page-load timings and your browser and device type. Cloudflare also receives your IP address, as any server does, and reports the visit's country to us. We only see totals, never individual visitors. The counter sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser (we checked in Chrome on 7 November 2026), and it never sees the contents of files you open in our tools. Ad blockers may block it; the site works the same either way. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Browser games

When you play a game on this site, it stores your progress (levels unlocked, stars, settings) in your browser's local storage on your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing your browser's site data. Some game demos are loaded from our older site, vpc-apps.github.io, which is also hosted by GitHub Pages and run by us under the same rules.

Free tools

Our tools at vpcapps.net/tools (such as CSV to Dashboard) process the files you choose locally in your browser and send nothing from them to us or anyone else; your files never leave your device. The tool pages count visits like the rest of the site (see Visit counter), and that count never includes your file, its name or its contents.

Sheet to Dashboard Report (Google Sheets add-on)

This section explains how the add-on accesses, uses, stores and shares Google user data.

Data accessed. The add-on asks for two Google permissions and no others:

  • See, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Sheets spreadsheets that you use this app with (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets.currentonly). With it, the add-on reads, from the spreadsheet you have open: the names of its sheets, the address of the used range and of your current selection, the spreadsheet's time zone, and the cell values of the sheet or selection you choose (the header row plus up to 5,000 rows), plus a second sheet of the same spreadsheet if you pick one for an Etsy report. It writes to your spreadsheet only when you click Copy KPIs to a new sheet: it then adds one new sheet named "Report KPIs" with the report's headline numbers. It cannot open any other file and has no access to your Google Drive, email, contacts or account details.
  • Display and run third-party web content in prompts and sidebars inside Google applications (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/script.container.ui). It is used only to show the add-on's sidebar and report window inside Google Sheets. It gives the add-on no access to data.

How the data is used. Only to build the dashboard report you asked for, in your own session: the cells are read by Google Apps Script inside Google, and the report is calculated in the add-on's window in your browser. The add-on makes no network requests to us or to anyone else.

Storage. The add-on stores nothing: no copy of your data, no settings, no logs. When you close the report window, the report is gone, unless you downloaded it (a file on your device) or copied the KPIs into your spreadsheet.

Sharing. We do not receive your Google user data, so we do not share, sell or transfer it to anyone. It is not used for advertising, and it is not used to develop, improve or train generalized or non-personalized AI or machine-learning models.

Limited Use. VPC Apps' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access. You can remove the add-on in Google Sheets (Extensions → Add-ons → Manage add-ons) and revoke its access at any time in your Google Account (Security → Third-party apps and services). As we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to delete.

Help page: vpcapps.net/tools/sheets-dashboard-report.

Release Readiness Scorecard for monday.com

The app is a board view hosted by monday.com and runs in your browser. VPC Apps has no server for it.

  • What it reads. With the single permission boards:read and your own monday.com access, it reads the board you open it on: the board name, groups, columns, and the items' values in the columns you map (such as status, release, priority, dates, assignee names, description, environment, files and reopen count). It does not read item updates, your account's user list or other boards, and it has no write permission, so it never changes your board.
  • How it is used. Only to calculate the scorecard on your screen. Board data is not sent to VPC Apps or anyone else, and it is not stored or logged.
  • What it stores. Per board, in monday.com's own app storage for your account: the column mapping (column IDs), the status labels that mean done, and the go/no-go rule limits. No item content and no personal data. Everyone who can open the board's scorecard sees these settings.
  • No tracking. The app has no analytics, advertising or AI features.
  • Files you create. A printed or downloaded report is a file on your device that can include item names, priorities and assignee names; you control who receives it.
  • Contact. We do not contact users of the app, other than replying when you email us.
  • Deletion. The stored settings stay in monday.com's app storage until you change them or uninstall the app; monday.com handles that storage. Questions: email us.

monday.com's own handling of your account data is described in monday.com's privacy policy. How-to page: vpcapps.net/release-readiness-monday.

CSV to Dashboard Report (Apify Actor)

The Actor runs on Apify's platform under your own Apify account. It reads the CSV file you give it (a link it downloads the file from, an upload, or pasted text) during the run, inside the run's container, and writes the HTML report, a dataset of KPIs and findings and a summary record to your own Apify storage. Apart from downloading the file from the link you give, it calls no outside services and sends nothing to VPC Apps. The input and the results are kept in your Apify account according to Apify's data retention for your plan; delete the run's storages to remove them sooner. Apify's handling is described in Apify's privacy policy. Please use only data you are allowed to process.

Videos and images

All videos, images and fonts are served from this site. We don't embed third-party players or social media widgets.

Advertising

This website does not show ads today. If we add ads in the future (for example Google AdSense in or around our browser games), we will update this policy before they go live, explain which provider is used and what it collects, and offer the consent choices required in your region.

Email

If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply, and we delete the conversation when it is no longer needed.

Children

Our games are made for a general audience aged 13 and over and are not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes and contact

We'll post any change on this page with a new date. Questions or requests: support@vpcapps.net.
