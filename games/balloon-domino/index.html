About the game

One tap. One chain. A whole sky of balloons.

Every balloon in Balloon Domino carries little arrows. Tap one and it pops, firing a dart along each arrow. Each dart pops the first balloon in its path, which fires its own darts, and so on, like dominoes in the sky.

The catch: you only get one tap. Find the one balloon that sets off a chain big enough to clear the sky.

What's inside

  • 400 puzzles in ten skies, from gentle Breeze to the final Eclipse. Every one is checked to be solvable, and most have exactly one right answer
  • Armoured balloons that need two hits, and bomb balloons that blast their neighbours
  • Double skies with two separate chains and two taps
  • Three stars for solving on your first try
  • A guided first puzzle and hints when you're stuck
  • Bright daytime skies or a starry night look (or switch automatically)
  • Short levels, no timers, plays offline; no account or sign-in needed

How to play

  1. Look at each balloon's arrows and follow where its darts would fly.
  2. Tap the balloon you think starts the biggest chain.
  3. Clear every balloon to win.
