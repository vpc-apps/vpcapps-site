About the game

The lantern festival is tonight, and every string of paper lanterns is a jumble of colours. Can you sort them?

Tap a string to lift its top lanterns, then tap another string to hang them there. A lantern can only go on an empty string or on one of the same colour. Fill a string with four of a kind and it lights up in a burst of sparkles.

What's inside

  • 200 puzzles, from three gentle colours to twelve-colour festival boards. Every level is solved by computer, so there is always a way, and the move target is the true minimum
  • A new Daily Challenge every day, with streak rewards: free hints, Golden lanterns at a 7-day streak and a rare Festival theme at 30 days
  • Hints that show the next move, undo and restart, and a bonus string when you're stuck
  • Three stars for finishing in the fewest moves
  • Colour-blind friendly: every colour also has its own symbol
  • Dusk and Daybreak skies (or switch automatically), floating sky lanterns and falling petals
  • No timers, short levels, plays offline; no account or sign-in needed

How to play

  1. Tap a string to lift the lanterns on top.
  2. Tap another string to hang them: on an empty string or on the same colour.
  3. Light every string with four matching lanterns to win.

Part of the Hoshikaze Valley world, home of Pop Pairs, Target Tiles and Balloon Domino.
