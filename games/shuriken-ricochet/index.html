About the game

Lord Onibi has stolen the spirit lanterns of Hoshikaze Valley. Only Ren, the young ninja, can win them back, one courtyard at a time.

Drag to aim, let go to throw. Your shuriken flies straight and bounces off walls and wooden posts. Line up the perfect bank shot to knock out every guard with the throws you have.

What's inside

  • 200 puzzles in five chapters: Bamboo Grove, Waterfall Shrine, Rice Terraces, Snow Pass and Volcano Fortress
  • 10 boss fights against Lord Onibi's lieutenants, ending with Onibi himself
  • Shield guards you must hit from behind, patrolling guards to time, and fire pots that blast everything nearby
  • Three stars for clearing a level on your own
  • Pon the tanuki gives you a hint when you're stuck: three free hints in every chapter
  • A Daily Challenge with streak rewards: free hints, a golden shuriken and the Lantern Festival look
  • Restore Hoshikaze Valley with the stars you earn
  • Short levels, no timers, plays offline; no account or sign-in needed

How to play

  1. Drag from Ren to aim; the dotted line shows the first bounces.
  2. Let go to throw. The shuriken bounces up to five times.
  3. Knock out every guard to clear the courtyard.

Part of the Hoshikaze Valley world, home of Pop Pairs, Target Tiles, Balloon Domino and Lantern Chain.
