About the game

Target Tiles is a math puzzle with a twist: the numbers sit on a board, and only neighbours can combine.

Flick a tile into the tile next to it to add, subtract, multiply or divide them. Your goal is to make the gold target number within the move limit. Simple to learn, surprisingly satisfying to master.

Five ways to play

  • Add: combine tiles with +
  • Subtract: always the bigger number minus the smaller
  • Multiply: watch out, anything × 0 is 0
  • Divide: only when it divides exactly
  • Mixed: pick + − × or ÷ for every move

What's inside

  • 240 puzzles: 48 in each of the five modes, each with a perfect-score "par"
  • Up to three stars for solving in the fewest moves
  • A guided first game in every mode
  • Undo and hints when you're stuck
  • Day and Night looks, or switch automatically
  • Plays offline; no account or sign-in needed

Great mental-math practice for teens and adults, without timers or pressure.
