About the game

Pop Pairs is a bright, bite-sized number puzzle. Swipe to slide every tile on the board. When two equal numbers meet, they pop into one: 8 + 8 = 16. Reach the target tile before you run out of moves.

Every level is a small, hand-shaped puzzle, not an endless grind. Plan a few swipes ahead, use your moves wisely and collect up to three stars.

What's inside

  • 120 levels, from a gentle warm-up to tough 512 challenges
  • Stars for finishing in fewer moves
  • Undo and hints when you're stuck
  • A guided first game that shows you how to play
  • Day and Night looks: bright and glossy by day, glowing neon after dark (or switch automatically)
  • Short levels that fit a coffee break
  • Plays offline; no account or sign-in needed

How to play

  1. Swipe up, down, left or right. All tiles slide.
  2. Two tiles with the same number merge into their sum.
  3. A new small tile appears after each swipe.
  4. Make the goal tile within the move limit.
