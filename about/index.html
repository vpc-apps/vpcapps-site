About VPC Apps

VPC Apps is a small independent studio making bite-size puzzle games. We started in 2026.

VPC Apps is a brand of Venture Path Consulting, an independent business registered in India.

Our first 3 games are live on Google Play: Balloon Domino, Pop Pairs, Target Tiles. Shuriken Ricochet and Lantern Chain are next, and you can already play their demos in your browser.

What we care about

  • Fun in the first minute. You land in a level straight away, with a hand showing the first move instead of a wall of text.
  • Short levels, no pressure. No timers, no energy systems, no sign-in.
  • Fair ads. Rewarded ads are always your choice. Ads between levels come at natural breaks, never in the middle of a move, and an optional purchase turns them off.
  • Honest growth. We never buy fake installs or reviews, and we never ask anyone to click ads.
  • Privacy by default. Your progress stays on your device. Each game has its own privacy policy.

Hoshikaze Valley

Most of our games live in Hoshikaze Valley, a small ninja world with its own cast: Ren, the young ninja; Mika, his friend; Pon, a tanuki who hands out hints; and Lord Onibi, who keeps stealing the valley's spirit lanterns.

How we make games

We design, code and test every game ourselves, and we use AI tools along the way. When a game's art or music was made with the help of AI, its store page says so.
