Turn any CSV file into a dashboard

Sales lists, ticket exports, inventories, survey results: drop any CSV table and get a general dashboard of its columns in seconds, with key numbers, top values, trends over time, number statistics, a data quality check and findings in plain English.

What you get

  • Headline numbers: rows, how complete the data is, the total and average of your main amount column, and the date range.
  • Findings written out for you, such as the most common values, how much the top 5 customers or products make up, and columns that are mostly blank.
  • Charts: rows by your most useful category columns, rows and totals per day, week or month, and the spread of your main number column.
  • Exact tables: min, median, average and max for every number column, counts for two categories side by side, and a data quality check of every column.
  • An Adjust panel to choose the date, the measure and up to 4 breakdown columns, plus a one-file HTML report and a print-ready PDF layout.

How to export the file

  1. From Excel or Google Sheets: File → Save As (or Download) → CSV. Most other tools have an Export → CSV button.
  2. Make sure the first row holds the column names.
  3. Drop the file above. Comma, semicolon and tab separated files (.csv, .tsv, .txt) all work.
  4. Open Adjust if you want a different date, measure or breakdown columns.

Your file never leaves your device

The tool is a web page that runs entirely in your browser. When you choose a file, it is read and analysed on your computer: nothing is uploaded, stored or seen by us. After your first visit it also works with no internet connection. The page counts anonymous visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics (no cookies, nothing from your file); ad blockers may block it. See our privacy policy.

Questions

Is my file uploaded anywhere?

No. The file is read and analysed by JavaScript in your own browser tab. Nothing from it is sent to us or to any server, and the tool keeps working offline after your first visit.

How does it decide what to chart?

It reads every column and decides what it holds: numbers, money, percentages, dates, yes/no, categories, IDs or free text. Then it applies a few simple rules: categories with 5 to 30 different values are charted first, a money column named like Amount or Total becomes the main measure, ratings from 1 to 5 are averaged instead of added up, and ID columns are left out of the charts.

What if it picks the wrong columns?

Open Adjust above the dashboard and choose the date column, the measure and up to 4 breakdown columns. Your choice is kept while the page is open.

Is there a limit?

The tool reads the first 5,000 rows of each file.

Those are recognised automatically and get a tailored report, with their own pages under More free tools below.
