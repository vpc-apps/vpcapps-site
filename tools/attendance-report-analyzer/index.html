Analyse a biometric attendance report in seconds

Drop the attendance export from your biometric machine (eSSL eTimeTrackLite, ZKTeco, BioTime) and get late arrivals, early exits, overtime, absences and missing punches per employee, with your own shift times and grace minutes. No manual Excel clean-up every month.

What you get

  • Attendance rate, late arrivals, average hours per day, overtime hours and missing punches for the whole team.
  • Your rules: shift start and end, grace minutes and weekly off days (Sunday, Saturday + Sunday, Friday or none).
  • Charts: people present per working day, arrival times in 15-minute slots, late arrivals by weekday.
  • Top 10 for late arrivals and overtime, and an employee table sorted by most late and absent.
  • Reads a punch log (one row per punch) or a daily In/Out report. A one-file HTML summary or PDF for HR and payroll.

How to export the file

  1. eSSL eTimeTrackLite: Reports → Daily Attendance (or Monthly In/Out) → Export to Excel, then in Excel File → Save As → CSV.
  2. ZKTeco / BioTime: Attendance → Transactions, pick the date range, then Export → CSV.
  3. Other devices: any export with an employee ID or name, a date and punch times (or In/Out columns) usually works.
  4. Drop the file above, then set your shift times, grace minutes and weekly off.

Your file never leaves your device

The tool is a web page that runs entirely in your browser. When you choose a file, it is read and analysed on your computer: nothing is uploaded, stored or seen by us. After your first visit it also works with no internet connection. The page counts anonymous visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics (no cookies, nothing from your file); ad blockers may block it. See our privacy policy.

Questions

Which machines and software are supported?

Exports from eSSL eTimeTrackLite (daily and monthly In/Out reports) and ZKTeco / BioTime transaction logs. Columns are found by their names, so most other devices that export a punch log or an In/Out report also work.

How are late arrivals counted?

An employee is late when their first punch of a working day is after shift start plus your grace minutes. Change the shift or the grace and every number updates at once.

How is overtime calculated?

Time between the first and last punch of the day, beyond the length of your shift; anything up to 30 minutes over is not counted. Days with only an in or only an out punch are shown as missing punches instead of being guessed.

Is employee data uploaded?

No. The file is processed in your browser and never leaves your computer, which matters for staff data. There is no account and no server.

My export is in Excel (.xlsx). What do I do?

Open it in Excel and use File → Save As → CSV, then drop the CSV here.
