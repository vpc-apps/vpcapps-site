See your true Etsy profit from your own CSV files

Download your Etsy Order Items CSV and your monthly payments statement CSV, drop them here and see what you really keep after transaction, processing, listing and ad fees, refunds and shipping labels, plus your best sellers and sales by month. No spreadsheet formulas, no account linking.

What you get

  • True profit after Etsy fees, Etsy Ads, refunds, shipping labels and your own product cost (enter it once as a % of sales).
  • "Where the money goes": sales split into fees, ads, shipping, refunds, product cost and what you keep.
  • Etsy fees and costs by type, so you can see what transaction, processing and listing fees really cost you.
  • Best-selling items, sales by month and sales by buyer country (from the Order Items CSV).
  • Works with either file alone; both together give the full picture. A one-file HTML summary or PDF to keep with your records.

How to export the file

  1. Order Items: in Shop Manager → Settings → Options → Download Data, choose Orders: Order Items, pick the month or year and Download CSV.
  2. Payments statement: in Shop Manager → Finances → Monthly statements, choose a month and Download CSV.
  3. Drop one or both files above (or add the second one later with "Add the other Etsy file").
  4. Type your average product cost as a % of sales to see profit after materials.

Your file never leaves your device

The tool is a web page that runs entirely in your browser. When you choose a file, it is read and analysed on your computer: nothing is uploaded, stored or seen by us. After your first visit it also works with no internet connection. See our privacy policy.

Questions

Which Etsy files do I need?

The Order Items CSV (what you sold) and the monthly payments statement CSV (what Etsy charged and paid). Either one works alone; with both you get sales, fees and profit together.

How is true profit calculated?

Sales, minus every fee and cost line in your Etsy statement (transaction, processing, listing and other Etsy fees, Etsy Ads, shipping labels, refunds), minus the product cost you enter as a % of sales.

Do I have to connect my Etsy shop?

No. There is no login and no shop connection. You download the CSVs from Etsy yourself and the numbers are worked out in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Does it work for any country and currency?

Yes. It uses the amounts in your files as they are, so the fees match what Etsy actually charged your shop, whatever your country.

Is there a limit?

The free version reads the first 5,000 rows of each file, which covers a year of orders for most small shops.
