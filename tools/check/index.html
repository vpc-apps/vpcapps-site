Check that your file never leaves your device

CSV to Dashboard reads your file inside your browser. You don't have to take our word for it: these three checks take about five minutes. Use a sample file or a harmless test file.

1. Cut the connection

  1. Open the tool: vpcapps.net/tools/app/ (visit it once while online, so your browser keeps a copy), or the Chrome extension.
  2. Turn on airplane mode, or in Chrome press F12 (Mac: Cmd+Option+I), open the Network tab and change "No throttling" to Offline.
  3. Reload the page and choose a file. The dashboard still appears, with no connection at all.

2. Watch the network

  1. Go back online, press F12 and open the Network tab.
  2. Click the clear button (🚫) to empty the list.
  3. Choose your file and wait for the dashboard. The list stays empty: no request carries your file, or anything else.

Two things you may see. Download shareable report can add an entry starting with blob:: that is the file being made in your browser's memory. The Try a sample buttons load a sample file from the tool itself. On the website, loading the page also reaches vpcapps.net and our visit counter (Cloudflare Web Analytics); choosing a file adds nothing.

3. See what the Chrome extension may access

  1. If you use the Chrome extension (coming to the Chrome Web Store), go to chrome://extensions.
  2. Find CSV to Dashboard: Instant Charts & Reports and click Details.
  3. It says "This extension requires no special permissions" and "This extension has no additional site access". It cannot read your other tabs or send anything to a website.

What these checks show

They show what the version you have does, on your computer, today; repeat them after an update if it matters to you. Chrome itself still talks to Google (for example to check for updates), and other extensions you have installed may be able to read web pages. If you download or print a report, that file holds figures from your export, and where it goes is up to you. Found something different? Tell us at support@vpcapps.net. See also our privacy policy.
